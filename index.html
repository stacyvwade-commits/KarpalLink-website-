01 / THE TECHNOLOGY

More than a wearable.
A connected platform.

KarpalLink Core brings together two independent NFC channels and an individually assigned QR code in one wearable design. The architecture is intended to support distinct credentials and services within a single form factor.

01

Two independent NFC channels

Separate NFC components can be assigned to different organizations or service providers, subject to their credential and system requirements.

02

One assigned QR code

An additional QR-based identification or access pathway complements the NFC functionality.

03

One wearable design

A compact wearable concept designed to bring multiple access possibilities together without requiring a traditional watch display.

Actual functionality, credential security, interoperability and access depend on the selected chips, software, partner systems and implementation.

04 / ABOUT KARPALLINK

Independent thinking.
Practical innovation.

KarpalLink is an independently developed wearable technology initiative focused on bringing multiple credential and access possibilities into a single, streamlined form. Its development is guided by a practical question: how might one wearable make it easier to connect with the services and experiences people use?

The KarpalLink Core concept has progressed through physical prototyping and component testing. The next step is continued evaluation with technology, manufacturing and potential integration partners.
