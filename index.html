01 / The platform

Designed around
how people move.

KarpalLink explores a wearable approach to access—bringing together contactless NFC interaction and QR-based access in a compact form.

The platform is intended to support different service environments through customer- and partner-configured credentials, systems and experiences.

02 / Product family

One platform.
Different possibilities.

A family of wearable concepts, each developed around a distinct use case.

Featured concept

KarpalLink Core

Dual NFC + individually assigned QR

KarpalLink Core brings together two independent NFC channels and an individually assigned QR code in one wearable concept. Intended services depend on the credentials and systems selected by the customer or partner.

NFC ONLY

KarpalLink Plus

A streamlined NFC-only band concept for everyday use.

PAYMENT WEARABLE CONCEPT

KarpalLink KOIN

A separate payment-wearable concept designed around an integrated payment module.

WEARABLE RING

KarpalLink Vow

A ring-form wearable concept: Validated Omni Wearable.

Product images represent design and manufacturing concepts. Final specifications, availability, integrations and supported services are subject to development, testing and partner requirements.
